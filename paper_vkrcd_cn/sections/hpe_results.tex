\section{HPE Results}
The HPE comparison contains 62 comparable rows. MPJPE improves in 34 rows, and PA-MPJPE improves in 33 rows. All HPE errors are reported in millimeters, and lower is better.
\paragraph{Important note on the Teacher.} The full-modality Teacher is not a missing-modality model. It is trained as a full-modality oracle with all modalities available, so evaluating it under single-modality or arbitrary missing-modality settings creates a distribution shift. Therefore, the Teacher may look very poor in many partial-modality rows, while its full-modality row is strong. The Student-VK and Student-NV models are the models intended for missing-modality robustness.
